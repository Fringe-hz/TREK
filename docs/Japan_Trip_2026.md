## Tickets and budget anchors

- N'EX Tokyo Round Trip Ticket: ¥5,200 per person.
- Hakone Freepass, 3 days from Odawara: ¥6,400 per person.
- Tokyo Subway Ticket 48h: consider for Oct 1–2; JR is not included.
- Fuji Excursion and G-LINER require separate reservations/tickets.
- Planning baseline for locked/priority tickets: about ¥19,350 per person, excluding local JR, buses, restaurant and attraction costs.

## Luggage strategy

Use hotel storage first, then official transfer, then staffed station storage, then a large locker. Avoid carrying the 28-inch case through sightseeing transfers. Reconfirm Fujiya early storage, Mikawaya arrangements, bus times, weather, ropeway/ship operations, and restaurant reservations before departure.
